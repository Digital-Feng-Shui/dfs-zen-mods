# DFS Calm

A calmer Zen for everyone, with nothing to learn first. Seven settings in Settings > Mods > DFS Calm.

## Settings
- **Selected tab.** Edge (the default): a thin ring around the tab you're on, with the same rounded corners as the Essentials, and a brighter name. Glass: the tab lifts off the sidebar like a pane of glass. Clear: almost clear glass that catches the light, with a rim that is bright at the top left, like the glass in macOS 26. Zen's own: no change.
- **Sleeping tabs.** Tabs Zen hasn't loaded yet, or has put to sleep, fade to half strength, with grey icons by default, so the tabs you use stand out. A folder fades too when every tab in it sleeps.
- **Tab text size.** One size for tab names, folder names and space names. 12px by default; Zen's own is about 14px.
- **Space name as a small heading.** The space's name heads its tabs instead of looking like one of them: small capitals, no icon, a little air above the tabs. Hover it and the fold arrow slides in; click to fold the pinned tabs.
- **Fade small buttons until you hover.** The compact mode, extensions and + buttons stay faint until your mouse is on their row.
- **Hide the link preview.** The address bottom left that shows where a link goes while you hover it. Switch it off if you like to check a link before you click.
- **Tidy top and bottom rows.** The stoplights, the top buttons, the space icons and the + share one 13px margin from the corners, with 12px above and below the Essentials, and Essentials icons a little bigger (18px).

Replaces the Ghost Tabs, Tab Text Size, Better Tab Indicators and Disable Status Bar mods. Ready for the next level? Add DFS Quiet.
